## description

Peaker is a logbook for people who climb mountains.

FIND THE MOUNTAIN
Search a worldwide catalogue by name, in any language, and narrow it down by country, region or altitude. Or let the app use your location to list the summits closest to you, with the distance to each one and a map.

LOG THE ASCENT
Record the date, who you were with and how the route went. Add up to three photos, taken with the camera right there on the summit or picked from your gallery. Note the snow, the wind and the state of the trail so the next attempt starts better informed.

KEEP YOUR HISTORY
See every ascent in one list, with your totals: how many summits, how many different mountains, your highest point and the last day you climbed. Each ascent has its own page you can edit or delete.

BUILD COLLECTIONS
Group the mountains you want to climb into lists of your own, and tick them off as you go.

YOUR PUBLIC PROFILE
Choose what the rest of the world sees. Every ascent is public or private, one by one, and your profile has an address you can share.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Spanish, French, Chinese and Arabic, with full right-to-left support.
